How to Fix Jio, Airtel & Vi Mobile Signal Problems in Pune

Are you experiencing 1-bar reception, muted voice calls, or "Emergency Calls Only" on your smartphone in Pune? Whether you are a Jio, Airtel, or Vodafone Idea (Vi) subscriber, indoor network dead zones are an immense frustration. Here is a practical diagnostic guide to identify the root cause and permanently restore 5-bar signal strength.

Step 1: Check Smartphone Configuration

Before looking at hardware solutions, confirm your device settings:

  • Enable VoLTE / Wi-Fi Calling: Make sure "VoLTE Calls" and "Wi-Fi Calling" (VoWiFi) are toggled ON in your SIM settings.
  • Reset Network Settings: On Android (Settings > System > Reset options > Reset Wi-Fi, mobile & Bluetooth) or iPhone (Settings > General > Transfer or Reset > Reset Network Settings).
  • Select Automatic Network Operator: Ensure network search is set to Automatic so your phone does not lock onto a distant, weak BTS tower.

Step 2: Measure Signal Strength in Decibels (dBm)

Signal bars are subjective and differ across phone manufacturers. Check true signal strength in dBm:

  • Android: Settings > About Phone > Status > SIM Status > Signal Strength.
  • iPhone: Dial *3001#12345#* to open Field Test Mode and check "rsrp0".

Scale: -50 to -79 dBm is excellent; -80 to -99 dBm is good; -100 to -115 dBm is weak (call drops occur); below -115 dBm is virtually a dead zone.

Step 3: The Permanent Solution — Cellular Signal Amplifiers

If signal readings outside your window or terrace are strong (-75 to -90 dBm) but drop drastically indoors (-110 dBm or worse), building materials (glass, bricks, RCC columns) are blocking the RF waves.
