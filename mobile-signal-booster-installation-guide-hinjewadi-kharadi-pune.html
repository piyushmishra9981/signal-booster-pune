Mobile Signal Booster Installation in Hinjewadi & Kharadi IT Parks, Pune

Pune is internationally renowned as an IT software, fintech, and manufacturing powerhouse. Tech hubs such as Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park in Hinjewadi (Phase 1, 2, 3), EON Free Zone in Kharadi, Cybercity in Magarpatta, and commercial towers along Baner-Balewadi house thousands of professionals whose everyday operations rely on flawless mobile phone connectivity.

The Challenge: Heavy Glass Facades & Underground Basements

Modern corporate towers feature heavy energy-efficient tinted glass facades and multi-level underground parking basements (B1, B2, B3). These structures completely block cellular radio waves from entering office floors and cafeteria spaces, leading to dropped customer calls, missed two-factor authentication (2FA) SMS codes, and unproductive work environments.

Our Commercial Distributed Antenna System (DAS) Solutions

For large commercial floorplans (5,000 sq ft to 50,000+ sq ft), standard single-room boosters are insufficient. We engineer High-Power Commercial Repeaters with RF power splitters and multiple indoor dome antennas distributed strategically across zones:

  • Server & Conference Rooms: Dedicated high-capacity indoor antennas for executive clarity during Zoom/Teams calls.
  • Open Workspaces: Ceiling-mounted 360-degree omnidirectional antennas providing uniform coverage for 100+ simultaneous smartphone users.
  • Basements & Parking Lots: High-output directional panel antennas eliminating subterranean dead zones.

Key Highlights of Our Pune Commercial Installations

  • Multi-Operator Support: Seamlessly amplifies Airtel, Reliance Jio, Vodafone Idea (Vi), and BSNL at the same time.
  • Zero Spectrum Interference: Equipped with Intelligent Automatic Gain Control (AGC) to comply strictly with DoT safety guidelines.
  • Turnkey Execution: Comprehensive site RF audit, cable concealment, mounting, testing, and 1-year on-site warranty.
